How the score works

Every market on the board is scored from 0 to 100 on the timeframe you choose. The score blends six things a trader would check by hand:

  • Trend: where price sits against the 9, 21, 50 and 200 period moving averages and whether they are stacked in order.
  • Momentum: RSI, MACD and the stochastic oscillator.
  • Volume: whether today's activity is above normal and who is in control, buyers or sellers.
  • Structure: higher highs and higher lows, or lower highs and lower lows.
  • Breakouts: fresh breaks of nearby support or resistance.
  • Higher timeframe: whether the bigger picture agrees.

Scores of 65 and above lean bullish, 35 and below lean bearish, and the middle is a range or a market without a clear direction. A high score means the conditions line up, not that price must keep going. Use it to decide where to look, then plan the trade with a stop loss and a position size you can afford.
