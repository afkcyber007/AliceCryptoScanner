The stochastic oscillator is one of the oldest momentum indicators, and still one of the most used on MT4, MT5 and TradingView. Used alone it gives many false signals. Used with the trend, it is a precise timing tool.

## How the stochastic works

Developed by George Lane, the stochastic compares the close with the range of the last periods:

<span class="formula">%K = (Close − Lowest low) ÷ (Highest high − Lowest low) × 100</span>

- **%K** is the main line.
- **%D** is a moving average of %K, usually over 3 periods, the signal line.

The common setting is **14, 3, 3**: 14 periods for the range, 3 for smoothing %K, 3 for %D.

## Reading the stochastic

- **Above 80: overbought.** Price closes near the top of its recent range.
- **Below 20: oversold.** Price closes near the bottom.
- **%K crossing above %D** is bullish momentum, crossing below is bearish.

Overbought does not mean "sell". In a strong uptrend the stochastic can stay above 80 for a long time.

## A simple trend strategy

1. Define the trend on the higher timeframe, for example price above the 50 EMA on the 4-hour chart.
2. On the 1-hour chart, wait for the stochastic to drop below 20 during a pullback.
3. Enter long when %K crosses back above %D and both rise above 20.
4. Stop loss below the pullback low. TP1 at the last high, TP2 at twice the risk.

For downtrends, do the opposite: wait for a rally above 80 and a cross down.

## Stochastic divergence

When price makes a higher high but the stochastic makes a lower high, momentum is fading. It is an early warning, not an entry. Wait for structure to confirm, for example a break of the last higher low.

## Stochastic vs RSI

Both measure momentum. RSI uses the size of gains and losses, the stochastic uses where the close sits in the range. The stochastic reacts faster and gives more signals. RSI is smoother. Many traders use one, not both.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the best stochastic settings?

14, 3, 3 is the standard and works on most timeframes. Scalpers sometimes use 5, 3, 3 for faster signals.

### Is stochastic good for scalping?

Yes, on the 1-minute to 15-minute charts, as long as you only take signals in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend.

### What does stochastic above 80 mean?

The close is near the top of the recent range. In a range it can mean a pullback is close. In a strong trend it simply confirms strength.

### Can I use the stochastic for crypto?

Yes. It works the same way on Bitcoin and altcoins. Because crypto trends hard, combine it with a trend filter.
