Yes, you can start trading with $100. You will not get rich from it in a month, and anyone who promises that is selling something. But $100 is enough to learn with real money, build discipline and prove your method before you add more.

## Step 1: choose the right account

- **Forex and gold:** pick a regulated broker that offers **micro lots (0.01)** or a cent account. On EUR/USD, 0.01 lots is about $0.10 per pip.
- **Crypto:** a major exchange where you can trade spot with small amounts. Futures are possible but need extra care.

Compare fees and spreads. On a small account, costs matter more.

## Step 2: set your risk per trade

With $100, risking 1% means **$1 per trade**. That sounds tiny, and that is the point. Your first goal is to survive and learn, not to double the account.

<span class="formula">Lot size = Risk in $ ÷ (Stop in pips × Pip value per lot)</span>

With a $1 risk and a 20-pip stop on EUR/USD, the position is 0.005 lots. Most brokers round to 0.01, so either widen the risk slightly to 2% or choose setups with a 10-pip stop.

## Step 3: pick one or two markets

Beginners do better with fewer markets. Good starting choices:

- **EUR/USD** for low spreads and calm moves.
- **Gold** if your broker offers 0.01 lots, but expect bigger moves.
- **Bitcoin spot** in crypto, in small amounts.

## Step 4: use a simple, written plan

Write down your entry rules, stop loss, targets and when you do not trade (news, Fridays late, after two losses). Follow signals or a scanner if you like, but always with your own risk rules.

## Step 5: grow it slowly

A 5% month on $100 is only $5, but on $10,000 it is $500. The habits are the same. Grow the account with deposits and results, not with bigger risk.

## Mistakes that kill small accounts

- Using maximum leverage "because the account is small".
- No stop loss.
- Trading every news release.
- Doubling the size after a loss to "win it back".

## Frequently asked questions

### Is $100 enough to trade forex?

Yes, with a broker that offers micro lots or a cent account. Keep risk per trade around 1% to 2%.

### How much can I make with $100?

Realistically a few dollars a month while you learn. The goal of a small account is skill and a track record, not income.

### Should I use leverage with $100?

Leverage only lets you open positions with less margin. Your risk comes from the stop and the position size. Keep it small.

### What is the best market for a small account?

Major forex pairs like EUR/USD have the lowest costs. Bitcoin spot works in crypto. Gold moves a lot, so use the smallest size.
