Gold is one of the most popular markets to scalp. It moves a lot, it is liquid during the London and New York sessions, and the spread is small at good brokers. The same speed that makes gold attractive also punishes traders who scalp without rules. This guide gives you a complete, simple framework.

## When to scalp gold

Gold moves most when London and New York are open. The best windows, in GMT:

- **London open, 07:00–10:00.** The first big move of the day often starts here.
- **New York open and the London–New York overlap, 12:30–16:00.** The highest volume of the day, and US data at 12:30 or 13:30.

Avoid the Asian session for scalping unless you trade small. Ranges are tight and spreads can be wider.

## Know the pip value before you start

On most brokers one "pip" on gold is $0.10 per ounce, and a standard lot is 100 ounces. A $1 move in gold is therefore about $100 on one standard lot and $1 on 0.01 lots. Check your broker's contract size, because some use different definitions.

## Setup 1: trend pullback to the 20 EMA

1. On the 15-minute chart, define the trend: price above a rising 50 EMA is up, below a falling 50 EMA is down.
2. Drop to the 1-minute or 5-minute chart.
3. In an uptrend, wait for price to pull back to the 20 EMA and print a bullish candle that closes back above it.
4. Enter on the close. Stop loss below the pullback low, usually $1.50 to $3.00 away.
5. TP1 at the previous high, TP2 at one and a half to two times the risk.

## Setup 2: London breakout of the Asian range

1. Mark the high and low of the Asian session (00:00–07:00 GMT).
2. After 07:00, wait for a 5-minute candle to close outside the range.
3. Enter on a retest of the broken level if it holds.
4. Stop loss back inside the range. TP1 at the range height, TP2 at twice the range height.

Fake breakouts are common. The retest rule filters many of them.

## Risk rules for gold scalpers

- Risk at most 0.5% to 1% per scalp. Gold can move $5 in seconds.
- **No trades two minutes before and five minutes after high-impact US news** (CPI, NFP, FOMC). Spreads widen and stops slip.
- Stop after two losses in a row. Come back in the next session.
- Take partial profit at TP1 and move the stop to entry after TP2.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best timeframe to scalp gold?

Most gold scalpers use the 1-minute or 5-minute chart for entries and the 15-minute chart for direction.

### What is the best time to scalp gold?

The London open (07:00–10:00 GMT) and the London–New York overlap (12:30–16:00 GMT). Volume and movement are highest then.

### How big should my stop loss be on gold scalps?

Usually $1.50 to $3.00 on the 1-minute and 5-minute charts, placed beyond the last swing. Size the position so that distance equals your planned risk.

### Can I scalp gold during news?

It is possible, but spreads widen and slippage is common. Most scalpers wait until a few minutes after the release and trade the move that follows.
