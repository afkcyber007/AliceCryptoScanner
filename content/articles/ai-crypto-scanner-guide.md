## What a crypto market scanner does

A **crypto market scanner** pulls live price, volume and derivatives data for every market and runs the same checks a trader would run by hand:

- **Trend:** are the moving averages aligned? Is price making higher highs and higher lows?
- **Momentum:** RSI, MACD and the speed of the move.
- **Volume:** is volume rising with the move? Is there a volume spike?
- **Structure:** breakouts of recent highs and lows, support and resistance.
- **Higher timeframe:** does the 4-hour or daily chart agree?

The result is a score, for example 0 to 100, where high scores lean bullish and low scores lean bearish. Instead of opening 300 charts, you open the 5 with the clearest picture.

## Where AI comes in

Modern scanners add **AI chart analysis**: you upload a chart or pick a pair, and a language model describes the trend, the key levels, the patterns and a possible trade idea in plain words. AI is excellent at:

- Summarising a lot of data quickly.
- Explaining what the indicators show, which is great for learning.
- Spotting patterns such as ranges, breakouts and divergences.
- Turning economic calendar events into "what if" scenarios.

## The limits of AI trading tools

AI does not see the future. It works from the same chart and data you see. It can be confidently wrong, especially around surprise news, very low liquidity coins or sudden whale moves. That is why the best way to use an **AI trading bot** or scanner is as a fast filter and second opinion, never as a promise:

1. Let the scanner find candidates.
2. Check the chart yourself on two timeframes.
3. Check the news and the economic calendar.
4. Place the trade with a stop loss and a fixed risk.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an AI crypto scanner make me profitable?

It can save time and help you find better setups, but profits depend on your entries, exits and risk management. No tool removes risk.

### Is an AI scanner the same as a trading bot?

No. A scanner finds and explains setups; a trading bot places trades automatically. Alice is a scanner and analysis tool: you stay in control of every trade.

### Which timeframe should I scan?

Swing traders scan the 4-hour and daily, day traders the 1-hour and 15-minute. Always check that the higher timeframe agrees.
