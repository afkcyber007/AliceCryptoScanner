Gold is the favourite market of many traders, and a **gold EA** (an expert advisor that trades XAU/USD) is one of the most searched trading tools online. But gold is also the market where badly built robots lose money fastest. Here is how to pick a gold trading robot that fits the way gold really moves.

## Why gold needs its own robot

- **Big daily range.** Gold often moves 20 to 40 USD a day, two to four times more than EUR/USD in percentage terms.
- **News spikes.** US CPI, NFP and FOMC days can move gold 20 USD in a minute.
- **Session behaviour.** Gold is quiet in the Asian session and moves most in London and New York.

A robot with forex-sized stops gets stopped out by normal gold noise. A good **XAUUSD EA** is built around these facts.

## Seven features of a good gold EA

1. **A real stop loss on every trade.** No exceptions.
2. **Stops and targets sized for gold**, often based on ATR (average true range) instead of a fixed number of pips.
3. **A news filter** that pauses trading around high-impact US data. Check the [economic calendar](site:app/#/calendar).
4. **Session filter** that focuses on London and New York hours.
5. **Adjustable risk**, so you can start with a small lot.
6. **A long tick-data backtest** with an honest drawdown figure. See [how to backtest an EA](site:learn/how-to-backtest-an-ea-mt4-mt5/).
7. **Live results you can follow**, ideally posted every week.

## Red flags to avoid

- **"No stop loss" or "never loses" claims.** Grid and martingale robots look perfect until gold trends hard in one direction.
- **Only a backtest, never a live result.**
- **Huge lot sizes on small accounts.** One spike can end the account.
- **Pressure to buy today.** A serious seller lets you watch the results first.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best timeframe for a gold EA?
Most gold robots trade M5 to H1. Shorter timeframes see more noise and spread costs; longer ones trade less often.

### Can a gold EA trade during the news?
It can, but spreads widen and price can jump past the stop loss. Most good gold robots pause around the biggest US releases.

### Which broker is best for a gold EA?
One with low XAU/USD spreads, fast execution and EA support on MT4 or MT5. IC Markets and Exness are popular with gold traders.

### How much do I need to run a gold robot?
It depends on the lot size and broker. A cent account lets you start with a very small balance while you learn how the robot behaves.
