Crypto trades 24 hours a day and hundreds of coins move several percent every day. That makes it an ideal market for scalping, if you only trade the coins that are really moving and you keep fees and risk under control.

## Step 1: trade only coins that are moving

Most losing scalps happen on coins that are asleep. Before you look for an entry, filter the market:

- **Volume today well above its average.** Money is flowing in.
- **A clear move on the 1-hour chart** (pumping, dumping or coiling tight before a move).
- **Enough liquidity** for a small spread. Avoid tiny coins with thin order books.

## Step 2: two setups that work

### The breakout

1. On the 5-minute chart, mark a range where price has stalled for at least 30 minutes.
2. Wait for a candle to close above the range high (or below the low for a short) with volume clearly higher than the last candles.
3. Enter at or just above the level. Stop loss back inside the range.
4. TP1 at the range height, TP2 at twice the range height, TP3 at the next 1-hour level.

### The squeeze

1. Look for Bollinger Bands that have narrowed to their tightest in hours.
2. The first strong candle that closes outside the bands with volume is the signal.
3. Stop loss on the other side of the squeeze. Targets as above.

## Step 3: fees decide if scalping pays

A scalp that makes 0.4% on price loses a large part of it to fees if you pay 0.06% taker on entry and exit. Use limit orders when you can, compare maker and taker fees on your exchange, and avoid very small targets.

## Step 4: leverage and liquidation

Scalpers often use futures. Keep leverage modest and always know your liquidation price. Your stop loss must sit well before it. A 10x position is liquidated after roughly a 10% move against you, minus fees and maintenance margin.

## Rules that keep scalpers alive

- Risk a fixed 0.5% to 1% per trade.
- Stop trading after three losses in a day.
- Do not scalp against Bitcoin. When BTC dumps, most altcoins dump harder.
- Take part profit at TP1 and move the stop to entry after TP2.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best timeframe for crypto scalping?

Most crypto scalpers enter on the 1-minute to 5-minute charts and use the 15-minute or 1-hour chart for direction.

### Which coins are best for scalping?

Coins with high volume and a clear move today: Bitcoin, Ethereum and the most traded altcoins on your exchange. Avoid coins with thin order books.

### Is crypto scalping profitable?

It can be, with strict risk management, low fees and patience to wait for real setups. Many beginners lose because they overtrade small coins with high leverage.

### Should I scalp spot or futures?

Spot is simpler and has no liquidation. Futures allow shorts and leverage but need a firm stop loss and a clear liquidation price.
