Before you study one chart, it helps to know what the whole market is doing. Is money flowing into risk or out of it? Are most coins trending up, or is everything falling together? A market sentiment meter answers that in one glance.

## What a sentiment meter shows

A sentiment meter turns many charts into one number from 0 to 100, usually drawn as a needle:

- **0–30: strong bearish.** Most markets are falling with momentum.
- **30–45: bearish.**
- **45–55: neutral.** No clear direction, often ranging.
- **55–70: bullish.**
- **70–100: strong bullish.** Most markets are trending up.

## Sentiment meter vs Fear & Greed Index

The well-known crypto Fear & Greed Index mixes volatility, volume, social media, surveys and Bitcoin dominance into one daily value. It is useful as a contrarian signal: extreme fear has often been a better time to buy than extreme greed.

A price-based mood meter, like the one in Alice, measures what prices are actually doing across hundreds of markets, updated every few minutes. It is better for timing trades within the day.

## How to use market mood in trading

1. **Trade with the mood, not against it.** When the needle is strong bullish, long setups work more often. When it is strong bearish, favour shorts or stay out.
2. **Pick the strongest pairs.** In a bullish market, the strongest bullish pairs usually keep leading.
3. **Neutral means smaller.** In a neutral market, breakouts fail more often. Reduce size or wait.
4. **Watch for divergences.** If the overall needle stays bullish but your pair keeps weakening, look for a better pair.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a good sentiment reading to buy?

There is no single number. Trend traders prefer to buy when the meter is bullish and their pair is among the strongest. Contrarian investors buy small amounts when long-term sentiment shows extreme fear.

### How often does the Alice mood meter update?

Every few minutes, from the 1-hour scanner scores of every market.

### Is the Fear & Greed Index reliable?

It is a useful context tool, especially at extremes, but it is not a timing signal on its own. Combine it with price structure and risk management.

### Does sentiment work for forex?

Yes. In forex the mood meter shows whether most pairs trend in one direction, and the currency strength meter shows which currencies drive the move.
