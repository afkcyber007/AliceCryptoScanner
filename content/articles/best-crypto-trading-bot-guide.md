The crypto market trades **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**. No human can watch it all the time, which is why **crypto trading bots** are so popular. But "bot" can mean very different things. Here is a clear comparison and what to look for.

## Three kinds of crypto trading bots

| Type | Where it runs | How it trades | Best in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grid bot | On the exchange (Binance, Bybit, BingX) | Buys and sells in a price range at fixed steps | Sideways markets |
| DCA bot | On the exchange or via API | Buys more as price falls, sells on a bounce | Long-term uptrends |
| Crypto EA | MetaTrader 5 with a CFD broker | Rule-based entries with a stop loss and target | Trending and volatile markets |

## Grid and DCA bots

Grid and DCA bots are simple and free on many exchanges. They work well while price stays in a range or keeps rising. Their weakness is a strong fall: a grid bot keeps buying all the way down, and a DCA bot can tie up the whole balance in one losing coin.

## Crypto EAs on MT5

A **crypto EA** is an expert advisor that trades Bitcoin and other crypto CFDs on MetaTrader 5. It uses clear entry rules, a stop loss on every trade and fixed risk, the same way a forex robot does. You do not give any exchange API key, and you can run it on the same VPS as your forex and gold robots.

## What makes a good crypto trading bot

- **A stop loss** or a clear maximum loss per trade.
- **Rules you understand**, not a black box with "guaranteed" profits.
- **Results you can check**, ideally live and posted regularly.
- **Adjustable risk** so you can start small.
- **Security:** if a bot asks for an exchange API key, never allow withdrawals on that key.

## Risks to know

- Crypto can fall 10% or more in an hour. Leverage makes this worse.
- Scam bots promise fixed daily returns. Real trading never pays a fixed return.
- Weekend liquidity is thinner, so slippage can be larger.

### What is the safest crypto trading bot?
There is no risk-free bot. The safest setups use a stop loss on every trade, small position sizes and no withdrawal permission on any API key.

### Can a bot trade Bitcoin on weekends?
Yes. Exchange bots trade 24/7, and MT5 crypto EAs trade on weekends if the broker offers crypto CFDs then.
