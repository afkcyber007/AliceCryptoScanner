"Do forex robots actually work?" is one of the most common questions new traders ask. The honest answer: **some do, most do not, and the difference is easy to check once you know what to look at.**

## Why many robots fail

- **Curve fitting.** The settings were tuned until the backtest looked perfect, so the robot only fits the past.
- **Hidden martingale or grid.** The robot doubles up after losses. It wins for months, then one strong trend empties the account.
- **No stop loss.** Every trade wins until one does not.
- **Fake results.** Screenshots from demo accounts or edited reports.

## What a profitable robot has in common

1. **Simple, clear rules** built for one market.
2. **A stop loss on every trade.**
3. **A long tick-data backtest** with many trades and an honest drawdown.
4. **Live results** that roughly match the backtest.
5. **A seller who stays in touch** and helps with the setup.

## The numbers to check

| Number | Strong | Warning sign |
|---|---|---|
| Profit factor | Above 1.5 (above 3 is excellent) | Below 1.2 |
| Max drawdown | One you can live with, shown openly | Hidden, or near 100% |
| Total trades | 200+ | Under 50 |
| Max losing streak | Short | Long streaks with growing lots |
| Live results | Posted regularly | Backtest only |

## How to make a good robot work for you

- **Run it on a [VPS](site:learn/forex-vps-for-ea/)** so it never misses a trade.
- **Start with a small lot** and increase it only after a few good weeks.
- **Do not interfere.** Closing trades early or removing the stop loss breaks the robot's edge.
- **Withdraw some profit** regularly.
- **Think in months, not days.** Every robot has losing weeks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a forex robot make money every day?
No robot wins every day. Judge a robot by its results over months, including its losing periods.

### How can I tell if a forex robot is a scam?
Watch for promises of fixed daily returns, no stop loss, no live results, and pressure to buy quickly.

### Is it better to trade manually or with a robot?
A robot follows rules without emotion and trades 24/5. Manual trading is more flexible. Many traders use both: a robot for one market and manual trades with a scanner for others.

### How much can I earn with a forex EA?
It depends on the robot, the market and your lot size. Higher risk can mean higher returns and larger drawdowns. Start small and grow the lot as the account grows.
