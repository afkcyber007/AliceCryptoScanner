## How the forex & gold engine picks a trade

The engine scans **28 currency pairs, gold, silver, the Dow Jones and the Nasdaq 100** every five minutes during the London and New York sessions. A signal is published only when all of these agree:

1. **Indicators:** about **30 indicators** (EMA crosses, MACD, RSI, Stochastic, Ichimoku, Supertrend, Parabolic SAR, ADX, Aroon, Vortex, Heikin Ashi and more) vote BUY or SELL on the **H1, H4 and daily** charts, weighted 50 / 30 / 20.
2. **Headlines:** today's and this week's forex and gold headlines must not point the other way.
3. **Economic data:** released and upcoming ForexFactory data for each currency must support the trade.
4. **Volatility:** the pair needs a healthy daily range — enough room for the second target.
5. **Entry timing:** the 15-minute chart must confirm (price on the right side of the EMA 20, RSI in a healthy zone).
6. **News safety:** no new trades 30 minutes either side of a high-impact release for that currency.
