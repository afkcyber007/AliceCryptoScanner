## How the scalping engine works

Every five minutes the engine reads the **whole Binance market — about 500 spot pairs and 500 USDT perpetuals** — and sorts them into four groups:

- **Already pumping:** up strongly on rising volume and holding the highs.
- **Ready to pump:** volume building while the range squeezes under resistance.
- **Already dumping:** down strongly on rising volume and pinned to the lows.
- **Ready to dump:** volume building while price rolls under the 50 EMA.

It also ranks the biggest coins by strength and adds today's crypto headlines. The best candidates then need one of two entry models on the **15-minute chart**:

1. **Breakout:** price takes out the 20-bar high (or low) with the EMA 20 above the EMA 50, RSI in a healthy band and volume above average.
2. **Squeeze:** a tight range resolves with a volume thrust of at least 1.6× — an earlier entry before the crowd.

## Targets and stop

Targets and stop scale with each coin's volatility (ATR): **TP1 = 1 × ATR, TP2 = 2 × ATR, TP3 = 3.5 × ATR, stop = 1.3 × ATR**. Perpetual funding is checked too: negative funding under a rising price is squeeze fuel, crowded positive funding is a warning.

Every signal that reaches TP1, TP2, TP3 or a further target counts as a win, also while it is still running. A loss is a signal stopped out before any target. Signals that expire without a target or a stop are left out of the win rate.
