## The futures formula

Every 15 minutes the engine checks the **most liquid USDT-M perpetual contracts** on Binance. A long or short is published only when:

1. **Bitcoin agrees:** BTC is above (for longs) or below (for shorts) its 1H EMA 50 **and** its 4H EMA 25.
2. **Three timeframes agree:** price is on the same side of the 15-minute EMA 7, the 1-hour EMA 50 and the 4-hour EMA 25.
3. **Real trend:** ADX of at least 25.
4. **Not too wild:** the 15-minute ATR is below 5% of price.
5. **Real volume:** the latest candle's volume is at least 1.5× the highest of the three before it.
6. **Momentum:** RSI between 50 and 70 for longs (30 to 50 for shorts) and a candle breaking the previous high (low).
7. **Confirmation:** the next candle must close beyond the signal candle.

Only the strongest setup of each scan is published — at most 3 an hour and 10 a day, at least 10 minutes apart.
