## Live rates for currencies, crypto and gold

The converter uses rates collected by the Alice server: central world currencies from a public exchange-rate feed, and crypto and gold prices from the same live market data the scanner uses. Choose the amount and the two currencies; the result and the rate in both directions update instantly.

## Mid-market rate versus the rate you get

The rate shown is the **mid-market rate**, halfway between buying and selling prices. Banks, card providers and money transfer services add a spread or fee on top, so the amount you actually receive is usually a little lower. Compare the quote you are offered with this rate to see how much the spread costs you.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often are the rates updated?

The server refreshes them regularly through the day, and crypto and gold prices follow the live market. The time of the last update is shown under the result.

### Is USDT always worth one dollar?

USDT is designed to track the US dollar and usually trades very close to $1, but it can move slightly above or below, especially on local peer-to-peer markets.
