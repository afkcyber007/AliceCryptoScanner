## What is an automatic trading robot?

An **automatic trading robot**, called an **expert advisor (EA)** in MetaTrader, is a program that watches the market and opens, manages and closes trades for you by fixed rules. It does not get tired, it does not panic after a loss and it does not chase a trade out of greed. Once it is running on a VPS, it trades **24 hours a day, five days a week**, even while you sleep or work.
