This policy explains what data we collect on the Alice Forex/Crypto Scanner website ({{site_url}}), in the web app and in the Alice Forex/Crypto Scanner Android app, and how we use it. The full app privacy policy is also available at [{{api}}/privacy]({{api}}/privacy).

## Data you give us

When you create an account we store your **name, email address, password** (only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form), **country**, **date of birth** (used to recover your password), the markets you are interested in and, if you choose to add them, your **exchange name and exchange UID**.

When you pay with crypto on the website we store the **plan, amount, transaction ID and the payment screenshot** you upload so our team can verify the payment.

Messages you post in the discussion rooms, alerts you create, and your coin and referral history are stored on our server so they appear on all your devices. Your trading journal and portfolio in the web app are stored **only in your browser**.

## Data collected automatically

Our server records basic technical information such as IP address, time of requests and app version to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.

## Cookies and advertising

This website may show ads from **Google AdSense** and other advertising partners. These third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and from many other vendors at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices). Visitors in the European Economic Area and the UK are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

We may use **Google Analytics** to understand how visitors use the website. It uses cookies and collects data in aggregated form. The web app uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember settings.

## How we use your data

- To provide your account, plan, alerts, coins and the other features of the app and website
- To verify payments and activate plans
- To send notifications you have switched on
- To keep the service secure and moderate the discussion rooms
- To improve our tools

We **do not sell** your personal data.

## Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the service (for example our hosting provider, Google for Play billing and push notifications, and advertising partners as described above), or when required by law.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile, Delete account) or at [{{api}}/delete-account]({{api}}/delete-account). Your personal data is then deleted from our active systems. Older copies can remain in backups for a limited time until they are rotated out.

## Children

Our services are not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
