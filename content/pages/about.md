## What we make

**Alice Forex/Crypto Scanner** is a market scanner for the web, Android and iPhone that scans crypto, forex, gold, oil, indices and US stocks on every timeframe and scores each market from 0 to 100. Around the scanner we built the tools traders use every day: Best Trades, Gold and BTC deep analysis, an AI chart analyzer, alerts, a whale and unusual-activity radar, market hours, a risk calculator, a trading journal, a portfolio tracker, a trading academy and live discussion rooms.

You can use Alice in the [web app]({{base}}app/), on Android, and on iPhone by adding the web app to your home screen. One account works everywhere.

## How we think about trading

- **Clarity over noise.** A score, a level and a plain sentence beat ten indicators you have to interpret yourself.
- **Risk first.** Every tool we build should help you lose less when you are wrong. That is why the position size and risk calculators are free for everyone.
- **Honesty.** Markets are uncertain. Our analysis is generated from market data, it is never a promise, and we will never sell you guaranteed profits.

## Where the data comes from

Prices and candles come from public market data of major exchanges and data providers. News comes from well-known financial publishers and the economic calendar from a public weekly export. Analyses are calculated by our own server from that data.
