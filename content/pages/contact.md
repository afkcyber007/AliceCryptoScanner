## Support on Telegram

Message our support team at **[t.me/AliceSupportTeam]({{telegram}})** for help with your account, plan activation, payments, coins, or anything in the app.

## Email

Write to **{{email}}**. Please include the email address of your Alice account so we can find it quickly.

## Before you write

- **Payment not active yet?** Website payments are checked by hand, usually within a few hours. If it has been longer, send us your transaction ID on Telegram.
- **Forgot your password?** Use "Forgot password" on the [log in page]({{base}}app/#/login). You can reset it with your date of birth, or contact support.
- **Want to delete your account?** Go to Profile in the app and choose Delete account, or use our [account deletion page]({{api}}/delete-account).

## Partnerships and advertising

Exchanges, brokers and trading education projects can contact us on Telegram about sponsored placements in the app and on this website. All paid placements are labelled as sponsored.
