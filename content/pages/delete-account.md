## Delete it yourself (fastest)

**In the Android app or the web app:**

1. Log in and open **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. You can also do it in your browser at [{{api}}/delete-account]({{api}}/delete-account).

## Ask us to delete it

If you can't log in, send a deletion request from the email address of your account:

- Email: [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) with the subject "Delete my account"
- Telegram: [{{telegram}}]({{telegram}})

We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by reply.

## What is deleted

- Your profile (name, email, country), password hash, settings and language.
- Your watchlist, price alerts, trading journal, portfolio, notes and coins.
- Your device notification tokens and login sessions.
- Messages you wrote in the discussion rooms are removed or anonymised.

## What may be kept for a limited time

- Payment records required by law for accounting (kept up to the legal period, usually 5 years).
- Backup copies, which are overwritten within 30 days.
